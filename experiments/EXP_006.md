## What we learned

Even with same train and test data, the model reached at best 0.72 macro F1 and 89% in accuracy, it still couldn't overfit when it clearly should be. 
But looking at the other numbers, it tells a little different story, macro F1 of 0.674 at t=0.50 rising to 0.744 at t=0.80 on the same data it trained on. Precision is climbing with threshold instead of sitting flat.
This shows that model is learning, can learn and the pipeline is clean 
The model still failed to detect 96 apnea windows which confirms the prev ([EXP_005.md](experiments/EXP_005.md)) which shows that some apnea windows are genuinely hard even to memorize and these are most likely the hypopnea events. 
The best way to confirm this is by parsing the respevt.txt file with 3 labels - 0: Normal, 1: Hypopnea and 2: Apnea and retraining the model
Most likely the model will able to differentiate apnea but struggle with Normal and Hypopnea
